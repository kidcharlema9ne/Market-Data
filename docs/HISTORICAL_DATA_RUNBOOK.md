## Binding historical entry rule — D-051 (approved November 6, 2026)

For the HISTORICAL STUDY, use the **opening price of the next regular trading session strictly after the actual SEC Form 4/4-A FILING DATE** from the quarterly bulk dataset. This is a calendar/session-date rule, not an acceptance-timestamp rule. **Do not require, fetch, or reconcile acceptance timestamps to calculate historical investor entry.** Never use insider transaction date, assumed reporting lag, or bulk ZIP publication date as a substitute. Preserve the filing date, transaction dates, and disclosure-level grouping. The historical rule supersedes older acceptance-timestamp entry language in this runbook and in master specification sections 5 and 15 wherever conflicting. The decision log D-051 is authoritative for this exception. Real-time scanner alerts may use timestamps separately; they must not change historical entry logic.

Before proposing methodology changes or blocking on data fields, read docs/DECISION_LOG.md (latest decisions, especially D-051) and docs/MASTER_SPECIFICATION.md; do not reopen an approved decision. ## SEC source and timestamp reconciliation

- Download structured SEC insider transaction archives for published quarters and the EDGAR master indexes spanning the exact study dates. Join by accession. Keep Form 4 and 4/A originals, rows, and amendment relationships distinct. For unpublished quarters use the indexes to identify originals and extract ownership XML. The full-year index had 176,396 unique accessions; 24,186 index rows were in the 2026 Q4 gap through November 5.
- Use submissions.zip for metadata candidate generation. It supplied correct acceptance times for all four original smoke-test filings. Across 11,923 code-P acquisition candidates in the published quarterly data, there were 7,559 single timestamps, 4,363 conflicts, and one missing timestamp. A single reported timestamp is metadata evidence, not proof it matches the original. Keep source provenance and compare metadata to a sample of original headers.
- Original SGML ACCEPTANCE-DATETIME is Eastern local time. Parse it using America/New_York, preserving DST. Metadata strings ending in Z are presented as UTC, but duplicates can disagree by four or five hours or more. Of 10 conflicting original headers checked, issuer-side metadata agreed in eight. One other metadata record agreed and one case matched neither. Never universally prefer the issuer record or apply a fixed offset. 1,064 conflicts straddled 09:30 Eastern. Resolve from originals and preserve unresolved rows with flags. Use original issuer/security details and a point-in-time security map when joining prices.
- GitHub Actions returned HTTP 403 from SEC. The workspace retrieved individual originals and the 1.57 GB bulk metadata archive, but the earlier workspace download stopped after 100 originals with a reported network-policy block. Use an SEC-accessible persistent runner, a truthful identifiable User-Agent, one shared throttle, resumable accession-based storage, and a manifest of missing accessions. Stop on 403/429 and diagnose. Use the SEC published ceiling of ten requests per second and keep an operational margin. Store verified originals once, outside Git. Reconcile their accession numbers and timestamps to the index.
